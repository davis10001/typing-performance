01 / THE MEASURE

ONE WORD = FIVE CHARACTERS.

Typing tests normally turn characters into a standard word unit: five characters equal one word for the calculation. Correct WPM uses correctly typed characters; raw WPM uses all entered characters.

02 / THE CONTEXT

ACCURACY CHANGES THE STORY.

A faster raw pace can come with more mistakes. Read WPM beside accuracy, and compare attempts taken at the same duration.
